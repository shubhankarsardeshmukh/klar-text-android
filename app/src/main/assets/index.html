How to use Klartext

A 1-minute interactive 3D tour: flashcards, contexts, spaced boxes, pronunciation and quizzes.

Say new words aloud

Pronounces a word the first time it appears. The speaker button shown while you study (and the Sound effects switch below) silences this and all sound effects.

Sound effects

Soft cues for right, almost and wrong answers, and when you finish a session. Muted when off.

Hindi meanings

Show the Hindi meaning on the answer side of a card, where the app has one. A second language to hang the word on.

Question types

How you practise a word. A new word always starts as a flip card.
